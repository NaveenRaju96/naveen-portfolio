GOVERNANCE · COORDINATION · ACCOUNTABILITY

When institutions work in silos

A proposed examination of what happens when services, data and responsibility do not connect around people's needs.

Social justice & civic action

Images in this collection provide context for questions about public voice, institutions and accountability.

Original field photographs and interview-based material will be added following appropriate ethical approval, informed consent and safeguarding review. These images are included for visual context and are not represented as original field reporting.

Editorial status: Analytical concept only. It does not allege wrongdoing by any specific institution.

The question

Public problems rarely fit neatly within one department. When organisations use incompatible processes, fail to share appropriate information or lack clear accountability, people can fall between responsibilities. A strong story would trace one evidenced service journey rather than rely on general claims.

What reporting could explore

  • How responsibilities are divided across organisations.
  • Where hand-offs, unclear ownership or duplicated processes occur.
  • What performance data, audit findings or official reports show.
  • Which coordination and accountability changes have evidence behind them.

Interview notebook

Questions for a future interview

Who owns the next action when a case crosses organisational boundaries? How are delays measured? What safeguards govern information sharing? How can service users challenge a decision or escalate a concern?
