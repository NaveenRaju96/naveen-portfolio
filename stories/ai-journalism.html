Technology, journalism and public trust

An overview of Naveen's undergraduate research into the impact of artificial intelligence on journalism.

Research summary: Confirm the final wording, method and findings against the submitted academic project before publication.

Research focus

The project explored the impact of artificial intelligence on journalism, with attention to trust, misinformation and ethics. It used a structured questionnaire with 50 respondents, convenience sampling, secondary research and descriptive analysis.

Why it matters

As newsrooms adopt new tools, questions arise about verification, transparency, accountability and the relationship between speed and accuracy. These questions connect technology with the public's ability to make informed decisions.

Research details

  • Method: structured questionnaire.
  • Sample: 50 respondents.
  • Sampling approach: convenience sampling.
  • Analysis: descriptive analysis and secondary research.
